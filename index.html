SAGE Math RFP review: executive view

The latest draft is materially more complete. It strengthens the consortium narrative, adds named practitioner sites and advisory members, clarifies the model architecture, introduces a revised evaluation-role split, and adds budget, AI-disclosure and prior-work content.

The submission is still not ready. The highest risks are: Q4, Q6, Q13 and Q14 materially exceed their character limits; Q3 retains a visible dataset TODO; Q8 remains incomplete; Q11 contains a major expansion of Safal’s role that should be formally confirmed and priced; and partner ownership is inconsistent across model build, validation, PMO/governance, pilots and stewardship.
